## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenDroneSMS](https://github.com/gearboxxsv/OPENDroneSMS)**  

  Anonymous Safety Reporting System (ASRS) for Unmanned Aircraft Safety Team (UAST). Reporting tool that can serve as a foundation to develop custom SMS programs for drone operations, prioritizing data sharing and privacy protection.



- **[SeparationEngine](https://roxanneardary.com/separationengine/)**  

  Open-source airspace computation platform licensed under AGPL 3.0+ that transforms aviation safety from reactive control to predictive assurance. Combines physics-based trajectory simulation, atmospheric modeling, and explainable AI to maintain safe aircraft separation through predictive computation.



- **[OpenCert](https://www.opencert.eu/)**  

  Open-source tool framework supporting modeling of safety standards and regulations (DO-178C, DO-254, EN50126, EN50128, ISO 26262), assurance project lifecycle management, compliance tracking, evidence lifecycle management, and safety-case modeling.



- **[OPENCOSS](https://www.eclipse.org/opencoss/)**  

  Open-source tool platform for evolutionary assurance and certification of safety-critical systems, based on Common Certification Language (CCL) extending OMG's SACM. Supports assurance/certification activities across automotive, railway, and aerospace domains.



- **[AMASS (Open Source Components)](https://www.amass-project.eu/)**  

  Open-source results from the AMASS project, integrated and maintained in OpenCert. Provides model-based safety assurance and certification technology applicable across aerospace and other safety-critical domains.



- **[Safety Data Collection and Processing System (SDCPS)](https://dspace.cvut.cz/)**  

  Research-grade open system for integrating occurrence and audit reports with ontology-based taxonomy management. Supports event/contributory factor chains, adaptive report forms, and safety performance indicators. Compliant with European occurrence reporting schemes.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ECCAIRS (European Co-ordination Centre for Aviation Incident Reporting Systems)** — European Commission database system for aviation occurrence reporting using ADREP 2000 taxonomy. While primarily a government tool, its data structures and taxonomy are publicly documented and referenced by open SMS implementations.

- **Open Aviation Safety Taxonomies** — Community-maintained taxonomies including ADREP, TOKAI, and ATM Lexicon for standardized occurrence classification and analysis.

- **Open Safety Case Tools** — Emerging open-source implementations of GSN (Goal Structuring Notation) and SACM (Structured Assurance Case Metamodel) for aviation certification evidence management.

- **Drone/UAS Safety Frameworks** — Open projects for unmanned aircraft safety reporting and risk assessment, building on ASRS principles.



**Frameworks for building custom aviation SMS solutions**: Combine **OpenCert/OPENCOSS** for certification assurance tracking, **SDCPS** architecture for occurrence-audit integration, **OpenDroneSMS** for anonymous reporting channels, and **SeparationEngine** for predictive safety analytics. For regulatory compliance, integrate ECCAIRS2-compatible export formats and ADREP taxonomy mappings.
