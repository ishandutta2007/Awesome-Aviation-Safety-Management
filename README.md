# Awesome-Aviation-Safety-Management

## Top Aviation Safety Management System (SMS) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Safety Reporting, Risk Management & Regulatory Compliance*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Aviation Safety Management Systems (SMS)**. These tools manage safety reporting, hazard identification, risk assessment, occurrence investigation, compliance monitoring, and safety performance indicators for airlines, airports, MROs, and aviation authorities.



**Examples** include Ideagen Coruson, Vistair SafetyNet, AQD, Baines Simmons Centrik, Yonder SMS, Comply365, Intelex Aviation, Qualtrax, ASQS, and SafetyLine (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom safety workflows, and transparent aviation safety data management — ideal for airlines, airports, regulators, and developers building vendor-independent SMS solutions.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Ideagen Coruson](https://www.ideagen.com/products/coruson/)**  

  Enterprise cloud software for complete control and real-time reporting of safety and operational risk across aviation organizations, serving 300+ aviation customers including tier-one airlines.



- **[Vistair SafetyNet](https://www.vistair.com/)**  

  Aviation safety reporting system driving real change in the management and investigation of safety-related occurrences, with mobile-first interface and offline reporting capabilities.



- **[AQD](https://www.aqd.com/)**  

  Aviation quality and safety management software for compliance monitoring, audit management, and occurrence reporting.



- **[Baines Simmons Centrik](https://www.bainessimmons.com/)**  

  Safety, quality, and compliance management platform designed for aviation organizations of all sizes.



- **[Yonder SMS](https://www.yondersms.com/)**  

  Cloud-based safety management system for airlines, airports, and aviation service providers.



- **[Comply365](https://www.comply365.com/)**  

  Aviation compliance and safety management platform with mobile-first design for operational efficiency.



- **[Intelex Aviation](https://www.intelex.com/)**  

  Enterprise EHS and safety management software serving aviation and aerospace organizations.



- **[Qualtrax](https://www.qualtrax.com/)**  

  Compliance and quality management software with aviation SMS capabilities.



- **[ASQS](https://www.asqs.aero/)**  

  Aviation safety and quality management solutions including SMS, audit management, and risk assessment.



- **[SafetyLine](https://www.safetyline.com/)**  

  Aviation safety management platform for safety reporting, risk management, and compliance.



- **[GfL Safety Management System](https://www.gfl-consult.de/en/software/safety_tools/airport_sms)**  

  Web-based SMS tool fulfilling EU Regulation 139/2014 and ADR.OR.D.005 requirements, with ECCAIRS2 interface, occurrence reporting, risk assessment, and process auditing for airports.

- **[SafeJets MS](https://www.safejets.net/)**  

  Aviation safety, quality, and compliance platform built for Part 135 operators and air tour operators, with hazard reporting, Bowtie risk assessment, fatigue management, and AI-assisted risk analysis.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenDroneSMS](https://github.com/gearboxxsv/OPENDroneSMS)**  

  Anonymous Safety Reporting System (ASRS) for Unmanned Aircraft Safety Team (UAST). Reporting tool that can serve as a foundation to develop custom SMS programs for drone operations, prioritizing data sharing and privacy protection.



- **[SeparationEngine](https://roxanneardary.com/separationengine/)**  

  Open-source airspace computation platform licensed under AGPL 3.0+ that transforms aviation safety from reactive control to predictive assurance. Combines physics-based trajectory simulation, atmospheric modeling, and explainable AI to maintain safe aircraft separation through predictive computation.



- **[OpenCert](https://www.opencert.eu/)**  

  Open-source tool framework supporting modeling of safety standards and regulations (DO-178C, DO-254, EN50126, EN50128, ISO 26262), assurance project lifecycle management, compliance tracking, evidence lifecycle management, and safety-case modeling.



- **[OPENCOSS](https://www.eclipse.org/opencoss/)**  

  Open-source tool platform for evolutionary assurance and certification of safety-critical systems, based on Common Certification Language (CCL) extending OMG's SACM. Supports assurance/certification activities across automotive, railway, and aerospace domains.



- **[AMASS (Open Source Components)](https://www.amass-project.eu/)**  

  Open-source results from the AMASS project, integrated and maintained in OpenCert. Provides model-based safety assurance and certification technology applicable across aerospace and other safety-critical domains.



- **[Safety Data Collection and Processing System (SDCPS)](https://dspace.cvut.cz/)**  

  Research-grade open system for integrating occurrence and audit reports with ontology-based taxonomy management. Supports event/contributory factor chains, adaptive report forms, and safety performance indicators. Compliant with European occurrence reporting schemes.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ECCAIRS (European Co-ordination Centre for Aviation Incident Reporting Systems)** — European Commission database system for aviation occurrence reporting using ADREP 2000 taxonomy. While primarily a government tool, its data structures and taxonomy are publicly documented and referenced by open SMS implementations.

- **Open Aviation Safety Taxonomies** — Community-maintained taxonomies including ADREP, TOKAI, and ATM Lexicon for standardized occurrence classification and analysis.

- **Open Safety Case Tools** — Emerging open-source implementations of GSN (Goal Structuring Notation) and SACM (Structured Assurance Case Metamodel) for aviation certification evidence management.

- **Drone/UAS Safety Frameworks** — Open projects for unmanned aircraft safety reporting and risk assessment, building on ASRS principles.



**Frameworks for building custom aviation SMS solutions**: Combine **OpenCert/OPENCOSS** for certification assurance tracking, **SDCPS** architecture for occurrence-audit integration, **OpenDroneSMS** for anonymous reporting channels, and **SeparationEngine** for predictive safety analytics. For regulatory compliance, integrate ECCAIRS2-compatible export formats and ADREP taxonomy mappings.
